AI assistant, on your terms

Integrate trusted AI providers for log analysis, code review, and incident response. For compliance and sustainability, you connect your own provider key and usage limits.

How it works

  1. Connect Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic in your admin console.
  2. Set usage limits, scopes, and workspace rules.
  3. Enable assistant modes for terminals, CI, and monitoring.

Assistant modes

Terminal helper Log summarizer Incident responder Runbook writer Compliance reviewer Stack optimizer
